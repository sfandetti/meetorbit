Harness Wiring

Your JST plug cables have four wires: black, red, white, yellow. The colours on these cables do not mean power and ground the usual way. What each wire carries depends on which socket it's plugged into. Everything below is read from the labels printed on your board.

The one rule: on the CN1 cable, red is a signal (IO22), not power, and yellow is 3.3 V. On the P3 cable, yellow is IO21, the screen backlight, so never connect it. Go by this page, not by the wire colour.

Diagram 1 · Power

The battery feeds the power board. Its 5V output goes to two places: the screen through the P1 cable, and the amp. The cable wires are short, so splice your red and black 22 AWG wire onto them (● = solder joint with heat-shrink).

Diagram 2 · Audio (mic and amp)

CN1 and P3 feed the mic. IO22 and IO27 also go to the amp, so those two wires split. The IO4 pad wire (plain white) goes straight to the amp's DIN pin.

Check before you power it

  1. P1 colours. Plug a cable into P1 and one into CN1, with no battery connected. Multimeter on beep: touch the black wire of the P1 cable and the black wire of the CN1 cable. It should beep, because both are GND. If it doesn't, the P1 cable is the other way round, so stop and send me a photo.
  2. 5V at the cable. With the P1 cable unplugged from the screen, put the cells in, press the button, and measure between the cable's yellow and black: about 5.0–5.2 V, yellow positive. Then cells out and plug it in.
  3. 3.3V at CN1. With everything plugged in and powered, measure CN1 yellow to black: about 3.3 V. That's the mic's power. Never put 5V on this wire.
  4. Splices. Slide heat-shrink on before soldering, and shrink it over every joint. The cut, unused wires get a wrap of tape each so they can't touch anything.
