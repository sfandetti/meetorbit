Privacy cleanup.

Select specific folders whose contents may be permanently deleted. The folders themselves stay in place. System, app and top-level personal folders are blocked.

Browser data — all local profiles

Close the selected browser before cleanup. This removes local data only; sync, backups and cloud copies are not covered. Cookies clear website sign-ins.

Permanent deletion cannot guarantee unrecoverability on SSDs or remove backups and cloud copies. Orbit does not claim forensic shredding.

YOUR SESSION

Keep your PC private.

Optional device passcode

Save changes to apply. Requires the passcode-capable firmware. The display relocks after one minute without touch; repeated incorrect attempts pause entry. PC command and cleanup buttons also require the passcode when enabled.

Lock keeps your apps running behind the Windows sign-in screen. Sign out closes your session and requires confirmation on the display.

The dedicated Privacy guard screen on Orbit has cleanup, Lock PC and Sign out controls. Hold REVIEW DELETE, then confirm YES on AI desk. Cleanup and sign-out requests expire after two minutes; hold YES on AI desk to confirm, or NO to cancel.
